FAQBlock · FAQs

What's the difference between a downloadable template and a TeamGantt template?

Downloadable templates open in Excel, Google Sheets, or as a PDF or Word file—no account needed. TeamGantt templates open as a live project inside TeamGantt, ready to edit and share.

Do I need an account to download a template?

No. Downloads are free and don't require signing up. Opening a template inside TeamGantt does need a free account.

Can I request a template that's not here yet?

Yes—tell our team what you're scheduling. New templates get prioritized based on what people actually ask for.
CTA wording follows format: Download for Excel/Word/PDF, Make a Copy for Google Sheets, Customize in TeamGantt for native templates (accent tag + accent button). Thumbnails are clickable — opens a preview with the same tag, title, and CTA, plus a "Best for" / "Includes" line. Only Excel Gantt Chart Template, Google Sheets Gantt Chart Template, and Simple Project Schedule Template carry real copy; everything else on the page is illustrative and needs real content before this ships. Construction and Marketing examples are limited to schedule-type templates only, since this is the Gantt Chart Templates page — non-schedule items (change orders, punch lists, plan documents) belong on other pages instead. Open data question: the Marketing "7 items" count is for all real marketing templates, not specifically the schedule-shaped ones — unclear how many of the 7 actually qualify for this page. Nine items previously shown under a Gantt Chart Templates section need a new home: Waterfall Chart and Waterfall Model fit Software Development once that ribbon exists; the other seven aren't cleanly industry-specific and would need either a dedicated cross-industry section or to appear in more than one ribbon — a content-modeling decision, not a layout one.

FAQBlock · FAQs

What's the difference between a downloadable template and a TeamGantt template?

Downloadable templates open in Excel, Google Sheets, or as a PDF or Word file—no account needed. TeamGantt templates open as a live project inside TeamGantt, ready to edit and share.

Are these templates built for residential construction, or commercial too?

These are built around residential and custom home projects first, since that's who we build TeamGantt for—but the schedule structure works for smaller commercial jobs too.

Can I customize a TeamGantt template for my specific trade or project type?

Yes—once it opens as a project in TeamGantt, every task, date, and dependency is fully editable, the same as any schedule you'd build from scratch.
