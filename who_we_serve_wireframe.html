Business and People are visually different weights on purpose: Business gets a bordered card with a photo placeholder, People is deliberately lighter — icon and text only, no box. The difference signals which is the bigger decision ("what kind of company am I") versus a quick filter ("what's my role"). "By Role" is worded broadly so a future Subcontractor or Client card slots in without the label needing to change — something narrower like "Your Team" wouldn't hold up as cleanly. Specialty Contractors is the more precise licensing/industry term for a contractor doing one specialty rather than general trade work. A real gap worth a decision: none of the eight personas speak to the client/homeowner side of a project, even though the client portal is a real, named feature. Persona descriptions are illustrative, written to match each role's likely focus rather than pulled from the live pages they link to. The two "Coming soon" cards (Construction Schedulers, Construction Business Owners) use a dashed border and no link rather than a live href. All three quotes are real, sourced from existing customer testimonials — Matt Pearson and John Raine's titles line up closely with Construction Project Managers and Construction Ops Managers respectively; Ollie Cooper's is included as general construction-industry proof without a forced persona match.
