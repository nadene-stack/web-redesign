Subcontractor Schedule Confirmations

Send subcontractors their scheduled dates and get a confirmation back—no login or app required.
Contact support →

Overview

When a task is assigned to a subcontractor, TeamGantt can send a confirmation request by email or text. Subs confirm directly from that message—no account, no app, no login. Confirmation status shows right on the Gantt chart, so you know who's locked in before crews mobilize.

Sending a confirmation request

Open the task, assign it to a subcontractor, and select Request confirmation. TeamGantt sends a link by email or text with the task's dates and details.

💡
Tip: Send confirmation requests as soon as dates are set, not the day before—subs need lead time to plan their crews.

What subcontractors see

The sub opens a simple, mobile-friendly page showing the task, dates, and a Confirm or Request a change button. No account required.

Viewing confirmation status

Confirmation status appears as a small indicator directly on the task bar, so you can scan a whole schedule and see who's confirmed at a glance.

StatusWhat it means
PendingRequest sent, no response yet
ConfirmedSub has confirmed the dates
Change requestedSub flagged a conflict—review before the task starts

ℹ️
Note: If a task's dates change after a sub has confirmed, TeamGantt resets their status to Pending and re-sends the request automatically.

FAQ

Does the subcontractor need a TeamGantt account?

No. They confirm directly from the email or text link—no login or account required.

What happens if a sub doesn't respond?

The task stays in Pending status. You can resend the request or follow up directly.

Can I require confirmation for every task?

Confirmation requests are sent per task, so you choose which ones need a response.
