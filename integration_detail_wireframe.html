Frequently asked questionsBlock · FAQs

Does this sync in both directions?
No. TeamGantt syncs schedule updates to Procore, so changes you make in TeamGantt show up there — changes made directly in Procore don't flow back into TeamGantt.
Will this create duplicate tasks in Procore?
No. TeamGantt maps to your existing Procore schedule rather than creating a second, separate one.
Can I sync more than one Procore project?
Yes. Connect as many Procore projects as you're actively running in TeamGantt.
What happens if I disconnect the integration?
Your TeamGantt schedule stays intact — disconnecting just stops future syncing, it doesn't delete anything.
