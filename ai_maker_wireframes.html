Can AI create a construction schedule from a project description?

Yes. Describe your project in plain language, paste your WBS, or start with a ready-made prompt. TeamGantt AI generates a live schedule with phases, tasks, milestones, and dependencies in place.

What types of construction projects can I schedule?

Residential and commercial—custom homes, spec homes, additions, remodels, office build-outs, tenant improvements, punch lists, and more.

Do I have to use TeamGantt after AI creates my schedule?

No. Download your AI-generated WBS as a CSV without creating an account, or sign up for a live, interactive schedule.

Can my subcontractors and clients see the schedule?

Yes, with a free Builder Edition trial—no TeamGantt account required for them to view it or confirm work.

Is the AI construction schedule maker free?

Yes, on TeamGantt's free Personal plan. A 14-day Builder Edition trial adds construction-specific features like subcontractor confirmations and cost-loaded scheduling.
